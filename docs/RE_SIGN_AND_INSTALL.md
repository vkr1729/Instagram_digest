# Re-Signing & Installation Runbook (Unsigned IPA)

This document provides complete, step-by-step instructions for signing and installing the unsigned `InstagramDigest.ipa` binary generated by the CI workflow (`.github/workflows/build-ipa.yml`) onto physical iOS devices.

---

## 1. Overview & Context

- **Artifact Source:** GitHub Actions workflow `Build InstagramDigest iOS App` -> artifact `InstagramDigest-Unsigned-IPA`.
- **Target OS:** iOS 17.0+ (iPhone).
- **Default Bundle ID:** `com.instagramdigest.offline`.
- **Signing State:** The IPA produced by CI is strictly **unsigned** (`CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO`). Because this is a single-user self-hosted system without an Apple Developer Enterprise account ($299/yr), the unsigned IPA must be re-signed locally with a personal Apple ID (free or paid) or installed using TrollStore/LiveContainer.

---

## 2. Obtaining the IPA

1. Navigate to the **Actions** tab of the GitHub repository.
2. Select the latest successful run of **Build InstagramDigest iOS App**.
3. Under **Artifacts**, download `InstagramDigest-Unsigned-IPA.zip`.
4. Unzip the archive to obtain `InstagramDigest.ipa`.

---

## 3. Installation Methods

### Method 1: Sideloadly (Recommended for macOS & Windows)

[Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io/) is the easiest and most reliable desktop tool for sideloading onto physical iPhones via USB or Wi-Fi.

#### Prerequisites
- A Mac or Windows PC.
- An Apple ID (your normal personal Apple ID is fine; a burner ID works as well).
- iTunes and iCloud installed (Windows only, non-Microsoft Store versions).

#### Steps
1. Connect your iPhone to your computer via USB (select "Trust This Computer" on the device).
2. Launch **Sideloadly**.
3. Drag and drop `InstagramDigest.ipa` into the large IPA icon box on Sideloadly.
4. Enter your **Apple ID email** into the Apple ID field.
5. *(Optional)* Click **Advanced Options**:
   - Signing Mode: **Normal Install**
   - Bundle ID: Keep `com.instagramdigest.offline` or use auto-generated.
6. Click **Start**.
7. If prompted, enter your Apple ID password and 2-Factor Authentication code. Sideloadly communicates directly with Apple to obtain a 7-day development provisioning profile.
8. Wait for the status bar to show `Done.`.
9. **On your iPhone:**
   - Go to **Settings > General > VPN & Device Management**.
   - Under *Developer App*, tap your Apple ID email.
   - Tap **Trust "[your Apple ID]"** and confirm.
   - If running iOS 16 or later: ensure **Developer Mode** is turned on (**Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode > Toggle ON** and reboot device).
10. Launch **Instagram Digest**.

---

### Method 2: AltStore / AltServer (Over-the-Air 7-Day Auto-Refresh)

[AltStore](https://altstore.io/) enables sideloading and automatically refreshes the 7-day certificate over your home Wi-Fi network.

#### Steps
1. Install **AltServer** on your computer and install **AltStore** onto your iPhone following the standard AltStore guide.
2. Transfer `InstagramDigest.ipa` to your iPhone (via AirDrop, iCloud Drive, or by downloading the artifact directly in Safari on the iPhone).
3. Open **AltStore** on your iPhone.
4. Go to the **My Apps** tab and tap the **+** (plus) icon in the top-left corner.
5. Select `InstagramDigest.ipa` from the file picker.
6. If prompted, sign in with your Apple ID.
7. AltStore will sign and install the app.
8. The app appears on your Home Screen. As long as your iPhone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi network with AltServer running, AltStore will keep the app refreshed.

> [!NOTE]
> Free Apple IDs have a limit of 3 active sideloaded apps at any time.

---

### Method 3: TrollStore (iOS 14.0–16.6.1 & iOS 17.0)

If your device runs a firmware version vulnerable to the CoreTrust bug (up to iOS 17.0), [TrollStore](https://github.com/opa334/TrollStore) provides **permanent installation without re-signing or 7-day expiration**.

#### Steps
1. Download `InstagramDigest.ipa` directly on your iPhone.
2. In the Files app or Safari Downloads, tap **Share** > **TrollStore**.
3. Tap **Install**.
4. The app is installed immediately with elevated privileges and will never expire. No Apple ID or computer connection is required.

---

### Method 4: LiveContainer (iOS 17.0+ Without Jailbreak)

[LiveContainer](https://github.com/khanhduytran0/LiveContainer) allows running unsigned apps inside a signed container app on iOS 17+, bypassing the 3-app limit.

#### Steps
1. Install LiveContainer on your device using AltStore or Sideloadly.
2. Open LiveContainer.
3. Tap the **+** button in LiveContainer and select `InstagramDigest.ipa`.
4. Launch Instagram Digest directly from within LiveContainer.

---

### Method 5: Manual macOS CLI Re-Signing (`codesign`)

For macOS users with Xcode command line tools and an Apple Development certificate installed in Keychain:

```bash
# 1. Unzip the unsigned IPA
unzip -q InstagramDigest.ipa -d /tmp/re-sign

# 2. Identify your development identity
security find-identity -v -p codesigning

# 3. Re-sign the app bundle
# Replace "Apple Development: Your Name (XXXXXXXXXX)" with your identity
codesign -f -s "Apple Development: Your Name (XXXXXXXXXX)" \
  --deep \
  /tmp/re-sign/Payload/InstagramDigest.app

# 4. Package the signed app into a new IPA
cd /tmp/re-sign
zip -qr InstagramDigest-signed.ipa Payload

# 5. Install onto connected iPhone using ios-deploy or Xcode
# Via ios-deploy:
ios-deploy --bundle /tmp/re-sign/Payload/InstagramDigest.app
# Or drag InstagramDigest-signed.ipa into Xcode > Window > Devices and Simulators
```

## 4. Troubleshooting & FAQ

### "Untrusted Developer" Alert
- **Cause:** iOS requires explicitly trusting certificates created with free Apple IDs.
- **Fix:** Go to **Settings > General > VPN & Device Management**, tap your Apple ID under *Developer App*, and tap **Trust**.

### "Developer Mode Required" Alert (iOS 16+)
- **Cause:** iOS 16+ requires Developer Mode to run sideloaded apps.
- **Fix:** Go to **Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode**, toggle it **ON**, and follow the prompt to restart your iPhone.

### App Crashes Instantly on Launch
- Verify that your device has internet access on the very first launch after signing, as iOS contacts `ppq.apple.com` once to verify the provisioning profile.
- Verify that your iOS version is 17.0 or higher.

### Certificate Expired (After 7 Days)
- Apps signed with a free Apple ID expire after 7 days.
- Re-sideload the IPA via Sideloadly or AltStore to renew the 7-day window. All app data and saved bookmarks stored in the SwiftData container will remain intact.
